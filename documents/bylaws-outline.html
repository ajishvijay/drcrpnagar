Draft outline

These are headings for the general body to fill in. They are not adopted bylaws.

1. Name

The name of the association, and the area it covers.

2. Membership

Who may be a member: owner, tenant, or both, and how a household joins.

3. General body

How often it meets, what notice is required, and how a decision is taken.

4. Committee

The offices, how people are chosen, and how long they serve.

5. Money

What may be collected, who may spend it, and how accounts are shown to residents.

6. Notices

Where official notices are published, including this website once the association adopts it.

7. Changes

How these rules themselves can be changed.
